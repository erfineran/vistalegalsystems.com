The platform

Everything a matter needs, from intake to signature.

Client portal

One signed-in place for each client to start a matter, see where it stands, share documents and pay.

Guided intake

Structured questionnaires turn client answers into a clean, consistent set of facts for the firm.

Contract Workspace

Agreements are assembled from the firm’s own attorney-approved templates and clause library, with every version kept.

Attorney review

Nothing reaches the client until an assigned attorney at the firm has reviewed and released it.

Review & sign

A focused signing step for the agreements the firm prepared: approved signers, in order, by secure email link.

Fixed-fee checkout

Firms offer clearly priced legal products, and clients engage and pay online in one step.

Secure messaging

Every conversation is tied to its matter, with firm-only internal notes kept separate from client threads.

Matter tracking

Configurable workflows move each matter step by step, with a complete activity timeline.

How it works

A clear path for clients. Full control for the firm.

  1. 01

    Choose

    The client picks a fixed-price product, or requests a quote for custom work.

  2. 02

    Answer

    Guided questions gather the facts the firm needs, before any drafting begins.

  3. 03

    Review

    The firm’s attorney prepares and reviews the document from its approved templates.

  4. 04

    Sign

    The client receives the released agreement, signs, and keeps everything in one place.

Built for law firms

Security-minded, attorney-led.

Attorneys in control

Templates, clauses and every released document are approved by the firm’s attorneys. The software supports their judgment; it does not replace it.

Private by default

Files are stored privately and shared through short-lived links, and every view and download is logged.

Strong sign-in

Multi-factor authentication is required for firm accounts and available to clients.

A record you can trust

Each matter keeps an activity timeline that cannot be edited, and finalized document versions are never altered.
